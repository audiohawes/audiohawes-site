02 / Background

Where it comes from

Audio Hawes started in 2015 in live sound. The shows are still part of the week, and they're where every one of these tools gets its first test.

03 / About

One person, Melbourne

I'm Dan Hawes. I work in live production, and I build the tools I wish I'd had on the job.

Most of them start the same way. Something goes wrong on a show, or takes three times longer than it should, and I go home and write the fix. If it holds up on the next gig, it ends up here.
